IPO Evaluator

Rule-based IPO screening with a mandatory historical validation record. Not investment advice.

Net returns at fixed horizons

Returns are net of spreads/slippage and include dividends, splits and delisting outcomes. "Excess" is versus SPY bought and sold on the same dates. Intervals are 95% bootstrap intervals clustered by entry quarter.

Buy recommendations: hits, false positives, missed winners

Capital-constrained account

$100,000 start, 5% of equity per position, at most 20 positions, 12-month holds, idle cash in T-bills. "Same trades in SPY" buys SPY instead of each IPO on the same dates.

Probability calibration

By IPO year, sector and market conditions (12-month excess return vs SPY)

Sensitivity and dependence on a few winners
